WHAT YOUR SUPPORT MAKES POSSIBLE

Real parts.
Real learning.
Real progress.

Building a robot takes more than a design. It takes materials, actuators, electronics, fabrication, and time spent testing and refining each system.

Support helps us work toward those needs while giving students hands-on experience across engineering, programming, design, and collaboration.

Our current work centers on Hatchling’s standing, balance, and expressive posing. What we learn also helps inform our longer-term Deinonychus project.
